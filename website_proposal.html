IOURMR Research Core Website Proposal 2.0

An external research website, hosted on GitHub Pages, to share our research products alongside the official university page.

What we are proposing

  • The university page remains the official source for our unit's identity, people, and contact information while SHAMP intranet hosts student resources.
  • A separate research site hosts what the university page is not built for: reports, interactive products, code, and student projects.
  • The research site is co-branded and links back to the university page.
  • Hosting on GitHub Pages is free and maintained by our team, with no university server or IT tickets.

The Need

  1. 1.Timely communication of research products
  2. 2.A searchable, accessible library of publications and technical reports
  3. 3.Transparent, reproducible science through shared analysis code, tools, and data
  4. 4.A repository of projects that demonstrates the work of trainees and students

  • Searchable by year, author, or topic.

Share the code, tools, and data behind our findings

  • Analysis code published alongside each paper.
  • Others can check, reuse, and build on our methods.
